Privacy Policy

Cymi · Last updated October 15, 2026

Cymi is a private iPhone app for composing outdoor outing journals from photos, route notes, weather, gear, and field writing. This policy explains what the app uses and how that information stays on your device. By using Cymi, you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Cymi is published as a local iPhone application (bundle ID com.cymi.app). There is no Cymi account, no sign-in, no sign-out, and no Cymi cloud sync. Journal work stays in this installation. For privacy questions, contact cymi.fieldnotes@gmail.com.

Information stored on this iPhone

Depending on how you use the app, Cymi may store the following in its private container on this device:

  • Photos you capture or import for an outing, plus working copies used to compose a journal plate.
  • Outing titles, dates, route and place notes, weather, wind, gear lists, and field notes you type or confirm after dictation.
  • Drafts, rendered journal plates, search indexes, and display preferences such as onboarding completion.
  • Your Trail Seal balance and a local purchase ledger, including Apple transaction identifiers used so the same purchase is not granted twice.

Cymi does not collect payment card numbers. Card details for in-app purchases are handled by Apple, not by Cymi forms. Location coordinates are not requested as a system permission; any place names you type are stored only as the text you enter.

Permissions

Cymi asks for system permissions only when you use the related feature. The first launch screen does not request Camera, Photos, or Microphone access. Declining a permission keeps drafts and other logging tools available.

  • Camera — when you tap Take Photo on the photo source screen to capture a picture for an outing journal page.
  • Photos — when you tap Import from Photos to choose a picture, or when you save a finished journal plate to your photo library.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — when you tap the microphone on a notes field so spoken words can become field notes after you confirm them.
  • Notifications — only if you allow optional outing reminders.

Cymi does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and does not use advertising SDKs to track you across other apps or websites.

Dictation

Dictation starts only after you tap the microphone control. Spoken audio is used to produce text in the current notes field. Cymi does not keep a separate voice-recording library. Transcription uses Apple speech recognition. When on-device recognition is available, processing can stay on this iPhone; otherwise Apple’s speech services may process the audio under Apple’s terms. You can always edit or delete the resulting text before it becomes part of a saved journal.

Purchases

Optional Trail Seal packs are consumable in-app purchases sold through Apple. Apple processes payment under Apple’s policies. Cymi keeps a local ledger so your Trail Seal balance is accurate and the same transaction is not granted twice. Unused Trail Seals stay on this installation. They are removed if you delete the app or use Clear All Data. Cymi does not offer a Restore Purchases control because Trail Seals are consumable and stored only on this device.

Sharing

Cymi does not sell personal information and does not share your photos, notes, or spoken audio with advertising networks. Journal composition, photo layout, and plate rendering are produced on this iPhone. The app does not send outing photos or field notes to a third-party generative service.

Apple may receive purchase and device information as part of App Store and StoreKit services. If you save a finished plate to Photos, or if you back up this iPhone with iCloud or a computer, those copies follow Apple’s backup and Photos rules. Opening this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use inside the app loads the page from cymi11.github.io; that request includes ordinary web traffic such as your IP address as handled by GitHub Pages.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a single outing inside the app. Clear All Data permanently removes journals, drafts, cached media, preferences, purchase ledger records, and unused Trail Seals from this installation, then returns you to first-run setup. Uninstalling Cymi also removes its on-device store. Copies you already exported to Photos are not removed by the app; delete those in the Photos app if you no longer want them.

Children

Cymi is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app to store photos or notes about a child if you are not permitted to do so.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of Cymi after an update means you accept the revised policy. See also our Terms of Use.
